title: 'From Street to Screen: Digital Marketplaces and the Informal Economy'
abstract: |
    Informal businesses such as street vending are a large source of urban self-employment in developing countries. In India, an estimated 40 million people sell food, produce, and household goods from carts and pavements, working long hours for earnings that depend heavily on foot traffic. Digital platforms have meanwhile reshaped retail and delivery for formal businesses. This shift has largely bypassed informal vendors while intensifying the competition they face, as customers move online. This project asks a simple question: can cheap digital access raise and stabilise street vendors’ earnings? We test a lightweight marketplace tool built around how vendors actually work, and ask whether it increases market access, smooths earnings across good and bad days, and enables demand pooling with nearby vendors selling complementary goods.
